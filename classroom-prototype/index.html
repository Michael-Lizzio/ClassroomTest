Chalkroom

A live classroom your students can draw on, and you can quietly answer. Teachers write on a shared board, students ask with a circle, and every question lands privately in the teacher's inbox.

See the classroom Watch a question being asked

Built for a founder review in under a minute: every demo has a Play button and a timeline.

What Chalkroom does

No accounts for students

Scan a QR code or open a link, enter a display name, and you're in the room.

Questions go to the teacher, not the board

Students circle any spot. The teacher gets a snapshot in a private queue.

Slides and captions in one screen

Present mode pulls a projector screen down over the board, with live captions along the bottom.
